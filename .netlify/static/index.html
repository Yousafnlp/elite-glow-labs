Case study

A clinic & wellness loungeacross two cities.

A multi-branch aesthetics clinic in Lahore & Sialkot needed to launch 10+ treatments with one premium, consistent look, and turn attention into WhatsApp bookings.

10Treatment campaign posts
3Cinematic treatment reels
1Booking-first website

The challenge

Every treatment looked different online. There were no clear prices or calls to action, so DMs piled up without turning into appointments.

What we built

A teal, cream and gold brand system. Each post pairs a bold one-word hook with a serif promise, a "from" price and a WhatsApp "Book now" button.

The reel formula

Concern → treatment → result → website → "Book your free consultation." Each reel runs about 18 seconds and follows one patient's story.
